Before you use Luagon

It's not a nuker

Luagon wasn't made to raid servers or cause harm. It's a Roblox utility bot.

Commands that look up Roblox or ScriptBlox information rely on those services.

The source is private

The code isn't public. It's stored on a secured computer and kept compressed and encrypted.

About Luagon

Luagon is a bot made by L0l kid and friends, inspired by Avis. Some commands are similar, some are different, and some aren't available. It was made to be straightforward to use, not to take anyone's work.

The bot is written in discord.js and Lua. Want to use Avis instead? Join their server. For Luagon news and help, join the Luagon server.

How to verify your Roblox account

Follow these steps in the Luagon Discord server to unlock commands that require verification.

Join the Humanoid CHT Discord ↗
  1. 01

    Join the Discord server

    Use the button above to join the Luagon Discord server.

  2. 02

    Open the Verify channel

    Find and open the server's Verify channel.

  3. 03

    Read the verification message

    Read the instructions in the message before choosing a verification method.

  4. 04

    Choose a verification method

    Select the method you prefer. Friend Verification is recommended by the Luagon team as the safest option.

  5. 05

    Open the username form

    Click the button for your chosen method. A form like this will appear.

  6. 06

    Enter your Roblox username

    Type your Roblox username into the form and submit it. Never share your password or account cookie.

  7. 07

    Check the bot's reply

    The bot will send you a message with the next instructions.

  8. 08

    Open Roblox and check your notifications

    You should see a notification that the bot sent you a friend request.

  9. 09

    Find and accept the request

    Open your Roblox friend requests and accept the request from the Luagon verification bot.

    Roblox may briefly show an unexpected mutual-friends count, such as “2 mutual friends,” if many requests arrive quickly. This can be a Roblox display bug; don't rely on that number alone. Check the account sending the request.

  10. 10

    Return to Discord and check verification

    Go back to the Verify channel and click Check verification.

That's it! Once verification is complete, you can start using commands that require a verified account.

Privacy Policy

Here is what Luagon Bot records when you use it.

Command usage counts

Luagon records command usage for security statistics, whether you use a slash command or a prefix command, in a server or in a direct message. The count increases when a command is used; it is not recorded with the Discord identity of the person who ran it. These statistics may be shared with the bot's staff. A retention period for these statistics has not been confirmed.

Messages and server activity

Luagon does not record full messages or conversations, and it does not keep a log of everything happening in your server. It only processes the command needed to respond.

Reports

When someone uses /report <username>, the submitted Roblox username is included in the report and can be seen by the bot's staff. The identity of the person who ran the command is not included in the command usage count. A report cannot be cancelled during its two-day review period; after two days, it is automatically deleted and cancelled.

Roblox and ScriptBlox

Commands that look up Roblox or ScriptBlox information may send the relevant command input to those services. Their own privacy policies apply to information they receive.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact the Luagon team through the Luagon Discord server.
